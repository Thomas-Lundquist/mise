# Vision

## The problem

Students often arrive at a cooking lab without having read the recipe. They spend class
time figuring out what to do instead of cooking, and they feel rushed and unsure.

## The idea

Before class, a student reads the recipe and enters what it asks for. mise turns that
into a **lab guide** they bring to class:

1. **Time plan:** each step with a clock time, fitted to the class period.
2. **Equipment list:** everything to pull before starting.
3. **Mise en place bowls:** ingredients measured and grouped by when they get combined.

Entering the recipe *is* the reading practice. The guide is the reward.

## What students learn

- **Reading:** pulling quantities, steps, and equipment out of a recipe.
- **Time management:** estimating how long steps take and seeing if it fits the period.
- **Professional practice:** setup, mise en place, working clean, and cleanup as part of
  the job.

## Who uses it

- **Students** (main users), at home or in class, on any device.
- **The teacher** checks a student's guide (on screen or printed). No teacher dashboard
  in v1.

## Goals

- Clear and calm to look at. Important information only.
- Truly usable for a real lab, not a toy.
- Nothing to install, no login, no personal data.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Accounts, class rosters, grades, or Canvas integration.
- Automatic recipe import (it would skip the reading).
- A recipe library or sharing between students.
